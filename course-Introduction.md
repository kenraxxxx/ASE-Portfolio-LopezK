# Course Introduction

The course CPELX230-330 introduces the concepts and practices of Agentic Software Engineering. It focuses on how artificial intelligence can be integrated into software development to create systems capable of analyzing information, making decisions, and performing tasks with minimal human intervention.

This course also covers Agile development, version control, teamwork, and responsible AI practices. Through these topics, students can understand how to develop software systems that are efficient, reliable, secure, and beneficial to users.
