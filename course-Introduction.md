
# Course Introduction

## CPELX230-330: Agentic Software Engineering

Agentic Software Engineering is a course that introduces the principles, methodologies, and tools used in developing software systems integrated with artificial intelligence agents.

The course focuses on how AI-powered systems can assist throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), including requirements analysis, software design, coding, testing, documentation, deployment, and maintenance.

It also emphasizes human-AI collaboration, prompt engineering, autonomous workflows, responsible AI practices, and the evaluation of AI-generated outputs.

Throughout the course, students will explore different software engineering practices and technologies, including Python, GitHub, Agile and Scrum methodologies, agent architectures, APIs, automated testing, and deployment tools.

The course also highlights the importance of professional engineering standards, ethical considerations, security, quality assurance, and human oversight when developing AI-assisted software solutions.

Through hands-on activities, projects, and real-world case studies, students are expected to develop practical skills in building, managing, and evaluating intelligent software systems.

## Course Objectives

By studying this course, I aim to:

- Understand the principles and foundations of Agentic Software Engineering.
- Apply modern Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) practices.
- Develop and evaluate software based on defined requirements.
- Understand how AI agents interact with tools, APIs, and software systems.
- Apply software quality and testing techniques.
- Practice responsible and ethical AI development.

## Expected Learning

At the end of this course, I expect to gain practical knowledge and experience in developing AI-assisted software solutions while maintaining software quality, security, reliability, and responsible use of artificial intelligence.
