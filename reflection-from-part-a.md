# Reflection from Part A

After completing the concept analysis, I learned that Agentic Software Engineering is more than just developing software that follows instructions. It involves creating intelligent systems that can analyze situations, make decisions, and perform tasks independently. I also realized that developing these systems requires continuous testing, collaboration, and improvement through Agile methods.

One important lesson I learned is that AI systems are not always accurate and can create risks involving privacy, security, and incorrect decisions. Because of this, developers must consider responsible AI practices and maintain human oversight. This activity helped me understand that building good software is not only about functionality but also about ensuring that technology is safe, reliable, and beneficial to people.
